# Privacy Policy — Space Tides (v0idgames)

**Effective Date:** November 2, 2026  
**Developer:** v0idgames  
**Contact Email:** gf.michelucci@gmail.com

## 1. Overview
Space Tides ("the Game") is a tactical sci-fi strategy game developed by v0idgames for Android, Windows, and Web. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can manage or delete your data.

## 2. Data We Collect
### A. Account & Authentication Data (Supabase Auth)
- **Email Address & User ID:** When you register with an email/password or sign in via Google OAuth, we store your email address, unique User ID, and chosen Commander Callsign to synchronize your profile, progression, match history, and purchases across devices.
- **Guest Mode:** You may play as a Guest without providing an email address. Guest data is stored locally on your device.

### B. Gameplay & Telemetry Data
- We store in-game progression metrics (level, XP, match results, unlocked units, implants, and cosmetics) linked to your User ID to provide leaderboards, cloud saves, and multiplayer matchmaking.

### C. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)
- The Android and Web versions of Space Tides display optional Rewarded Ads and post-match Interstitial Ads powered by **Google AdMob**.
- Google AdMob may collect and process your device **Advertising ID (AAID)**, approximate location (IP-based), and ad interaction diagnostics to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
- You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads**).

### D. Purchases & Payments (Stripe)
- Optional in-game store transactions are processed securely through **Stripe**. Space Tides does not store your credit card numbers or banking credentials on our servers.

## 3. Data Security
All data transmitted between the Game, Supabase cloud servers, Google AdMob, and Stripe is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

## 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion
You have full control over your account and personal data:
- **In-App Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud and local data at any time inside the game by opening **Profile > Overview > Reset / Delete Data**.
- **Email Request:** You may also request complete account and data deletion by emailing **gf.michelucci@gmail.com** with the email address associated with your account. Requests are processed within 7 days.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Space Tides is intended for audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
