# 🌌 Game Lore & Factions

## The Cosmic Tides
In the deep reaches of the uncharted cosmos, massive gravitational anomalies known as the **Space Tides** oscillate through star systems, leaving raw exotic energy, spatial distortions, and abandoned fleets in their wake.

Captains and rogue commanders from across the galaxy assemble custom battle fleets, seeking to harvest these anomalous energy signatures and establish orbital dominance over celestial sectors.

## Tactical Mechanics
* **Autonomous Fleet Deployments**: Position your ships on the tactical grid prior to engagement. Once the battle begins, ship AI systems, targeting priorities, and tactical implants execute based on pre-battle fleet formulation.
* **Component Synergies**: Shields absorb particle bombardment, kinetic cannons shred unarmored hulls, and specialized tactical modules buff allied formations.
* **Pilot Progression**: Sync your pilot records via Supabase cloud infrastructure to unlock implants, new hull blueprints, and rank across interstellar leaderboards.
